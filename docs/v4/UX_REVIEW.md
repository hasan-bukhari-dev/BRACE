## Words, color and motion

Warm porcelain with charcoal/cobalt and neutral graphite with adjusted text/controls use the same hierarchy. Five optically quieter wordmark hues belong to navigation. Data source/target and outcome hues have independent tokens; text, circles/squares, crosses and underlines carry meaning without color. The interlock favicon is a local SVG, not a generated scientific image.

Duplicate introductions were removed or disclosed. “14 /20” keeps the frozen family visible; “Threshold not met” does not imply no effect. “Each mark is one saved prediction” is used only for the actual per-record field; aggregated bins have their own count legend. “Not performed” remains absent computation. Class-only inputs say confidence is unavailable.

The measured plate retargets a 200 ms eased transition without moving letters. Pressed controls respond immediately; the home/header movement preserves orientation; dialogs reveal within 200ms. Reduced motion removes these effects without changing state. No simulated progress, confetti, randomized marks or animated scientific totals were added.

The conservative source-string inventory contains internal and generated literals as well as visible copy. It is coverage evidence, not proof that every sentence is comprehensible. The contextual decisions above and actual screenshots are the product review.

## Critique, repair and three remaining objections

1. **“A novice still needs too much input knowledge.”** Classification now has a guided declaration; biological assay declarations remain mandatory because automatically inferring organism, processing, independence or equivalent phenotypes would fabricate provenance. The public example supplies a complete working route. Usability of private-data preparation remains an unfamiliar-user acceptance item.
2. **“The dense scientific text and marks may be too small on a projector.”** Main plots and actions fit desktop viewports; all methods and exact data are retained. Narrow and native 200% zoom reflow works, with scrolling for long results. Physical projector readability and real low-vision/screen-reader use were not tested.
3. **“The new visuals could still make exploratory evidence seem stronger.”** The20-gene denominator,14/5/1 split, separate expression scales, pointwise interval caveat, unverified donor independence and processing differences remain visible or directly disclosed. The independent numeric audit passed. No clinical, causal, calibrated-confidence or locked-validation claim is supported.

Observed defects repaired during implementation: a JavaScript template-string startup error; false withheld styling for score-less records; misleading per-mark wording on aggregated bins; a partly clipped cohort chart; a selected-gene trace empty state without a direct return action; and different SVG viewport heights that misaligned paired-effect marks and axis labels. A final screen-coordinate test now verifies all40 positions across both themes and four widths. A native-zoom screenshot clipping defect in the test driver was repaired by capturing the actual Chrome viewport directly. A case-sensitive browser assertion was corrected to match the legitimate lowercase empty-state copy. These failed development attempts are not counted as passing release checks.

Still unverified: two unfamiliar users without coaching, physical touch hardware, narrator/screen-reader reading order and announcements, event-machine/clean-OS setup, and projector/monitor readability. Browser accessibility names and keyboard tests do not replace them. Engineering PASS is conditional product acceptance, not a claim of “Nintendo/Microsoft quality” demonstrated by human testing.

Optional outside services: DEFERRED. Pinned local gene annotation, existing examples and offline assets are sufficient. No cloud, AI calls, accounts, framework replacement or new runtime package was introduced.
