Quantile mapping produced lower cohort detectability (AUROC ≈ 0.515) but lower classification macro F1 (≈ 0.883) than selected gene location–scale correction (AUROC ≈ 0.688, F1 ≈ 0.903). Erasing cohort differences is not itself validation of biological discrimination.

Limitations

These are historical development-cohort comparisons, not independent locked validation, and cohort AUROC was assessed on a specific cohort-detection evaluation. They do not prove that adaptation caused improvements or generalize to all biological datasets.
