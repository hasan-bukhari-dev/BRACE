# Skeptical judging panel — answers grounded in the release

These are internal adversarial questions, not claims that a real judge panel evaluated BRACE.

|Question|Answer|
|---|---|
|Why does this matter biologically?|Study composition, phenotype ascertainment and measurement processing can change an observed association. A second cohort can challenge that evidence. BRACE retains nonsupport and design limits so a researcher can assess what survived the test.|
|Useful beyond CancerShift?|The intake contracts accept eligible user microarrays or labeled prediction tables; RACE and Mode A do not require the original CancerShift classifier. Applicability is bounded by supported assays/designs.|
|Why not R or Python?|You can implement these analyses there. BRACE does not claim better statistics; its value is compatibility review, frozen-source boundaries, linked records and exports in a focused guided workflow. No measured time-saving claim yet.|
|Why not GEO2R?|GEO2R supports differential comparisons inside a GEO Series. BRACE's specific implemented workflow freezes a source-selected family before testing it in another eligible cohort, then links molecular inspection/provenance. It does not replace GEO2R or limma. [NCBI documentation](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/geo2r.html).|
|Why not Galaxy?|Galaxy already has broad reproducible workflows and shareable histories. BRACE offers a smaller local cross-cohort research path; it has much less assay/pipeline breadth. [Galaxy reproduction tutorial](https://training.galaxyproject.org/training-material/topics/introduction/tutorials/galaxy-reproduce/tutorial.html).|
|Why not Evidently/model monitoring?|Evidently already evaluates, tests and monitors data/models. BRACE's implemented distinction is the biological cohort/phenotype compatibility and frozen gene-testing workflow linked to measured features. No claim that monitoring tools cannot be extended similarly. [Official overview](https://docs.evidentlyai.com/introduction).|
|What is new?|The post-baseline pass lineage adds verified public preparation, the RACE engine, linked FACE/ACE/TRACE workflows and strengthened EMBRACE/report/QA/release integration. Prior baseline engines and CancerShift research are disclosed. Exact competition-window authorship is not authenticated by file timestamps.|
|CancerShift predates the event?|Yes. Original research, classifier evidence and earlier code/planning are pre-existing. See the contribution register. Do not say every line was written during Hatchathon.|
|Are CPTAC predictions genuinely historical?|They are the preserved supplied historical records, with hashes and680/668 rescoring checks. The original weights/inference were not reconstructed; authenticity of original acquisition is beyond a hash check.|
|What was newly computed from public data?|Actual deposited GSE42568/GSE20711 expression was prepared reproducibly, then source-screened and target-tested by RACE. Final acceptance also performed fresh browser uploads/computations; saved original results remain separately labeled.|
|Why is gene replication defensible?|Source-only feature preparation/selection, saved frozen family, target tests over that entire family, exact groups and inclusion rules, independent numeric references and complete negative outcomes. Eligibility is exploratory, with ER/processing/independence limitations.|
|How is independence assessed?|Recorded acquisition/IDs, overlap and design checks; known overlap/repeated-donor designs can block. Missing IDs cannot prove independence, and a user's declaration is not authentication. The public pair remains exploratory.|
|Missing classes?|Observed-class balanced accuracy/macroF1 expose their denominators; absent true classes remain unevaluated. CPTAC has no true prostate support, so it is not complete five-class validation.|
|What prevents target leakage?|RACE discovery takes source inputs only; selection is frozen/saved before target tests. Baseline transforms/training/CV use source only. Permutation/change attacks and group-fold tests execute in the suite. This does not prevent a human choosing cohorts after seeing outcomes; disclose that research history.|
|How do you know shift harms performance?|We do not infer that from shift alone. Labeled same-run predictions can support descriptive error/shift associations, not causality. Unlabeled Mode C makes no accuracy claim.|
|What is the confidence slider?|A threshold over supplied maximum class probabilities and a saved curve; equality retained. It changes retained coverage, never retrains or proves calibration. Nonreference settings viewed after labels are exploratory.|
|Does BRACE explain biological causes of errors?|No. A sample/error and measured-feature inspection can guide investigation; confounding, processing and biological causes are not identified causally. Archived CPTAC errors are never assigned unrelated ER measurements.|
|Incompatible data?|Contract/design/measurement violations block, with actionable details. Processing or unknown independence can restrict an otherwise eligible design to exploratory warnings. Syntax acceptance is not blanket biological approval.|
|Unsupported workflows?|RawFASTQ/CEL alignment/reprocessing, generalRNA-seq count inference, paired/covariate testing, survival, single-cell/spatial/methylation modalities, automatic batch correction, arbitrary uploaded executable models, clinical diagnosis and cloud multiuser deployment.|
|How is it reproduced?|Use matching hashed inputs and exact requirements/configuration, deterministic public preparation, the frozen signature and saved methods. Numerical files are checked across repeated runs; software-version changes can change provenance hashes even when numbers agree. Exports exclude raw uploads.|
|Evidence that users need this?|The motivating research problem is authentic; no completed unfamiliar-user or customer-discovery study is claimed. Three neutral tasks and an anonymous results template are prepared. Demand and measured usability remain open.|
|Realistic future integrations?|Potential R/Bioconductor workflow interoperability or richer cohort annotations, subject to design validation. None is promised or implemented in this release. Do not describe a roadmap as current functionality.|
|Clinically deployable?|No. No prospective clinical validation, safety certification, harmonized phenotype assurance or regulatory evaluation. Research use only.|
|Without internet?|Installed app/assets/data/examples execute locally. Fresh installation needs compatible Python and either online setup or the matching prepared wheelhouse. No CDN/account/API key is required. A Python executable is not bundled in the source release.|

## Competitive positioning — hypotheses, not claims about other teams

No competing team's implementation, users, results or scores were inspected. The comparisons below concern plausible project types, and are strategic hypotheses for the presenter.

|Plausible project type|Potential strength to respect|How to explain BRACE honestly|Evidence needed before claiming superiority|
|---|---|---|---|
|Rural-healthcare administration automation|Direct, understandable service/workload problem; potential operational value|BRACE addresses the research evidence upstream of scientific conclusions. Show a real result and its caveats instead of implying healthcare administration matters less.|Actual team solution, adoption data, validated workflow outcomes and judging criteria; none known.|
|Epigenomics analysis/discovery|Rich biological questions and potentially deep assay expertise|BRACE currently handles processed bulk-expression/prediction evidence, not epigenomics. Its contribution is disciplined cross-cohort testing/inspection within that narrower scope.|Their actual assay methods, reproducibility and novelty; no invented weakness or dataset claim.|

BRACE is substantial bioinformatics because it actually parses scientific matrices and phenotype metadata, enforces compatibility/design restrictions, performs source-only statistical selection and frozen-family target testing, preserves feature mapping/exclusions, evaluates predictions with missing-class/uncertainty limits, and exports the numerical evidence. Its interface exposes those computations. It is not distinguished by decorative charts or a novel generic “reliability score.”
