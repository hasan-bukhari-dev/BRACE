## 30 seconds — unfamiliar expo visitor

A biological finding can look convincing in one dataset and weaken in another. BRACE helps researchers check that. It selects genes in one cohort, freezes that selection, and tests them in a second. You can inspect the measurements and export the evidence. It also investigates model errors. This is research software, not a clinical diagnostic product.

## Research-focused explanation

BRACE combines validated intake contracts with exploratory source-only microarray gene screening, frozen-family target Welch/BH testing, measured-feature inspection, supplied-prediction or binary-baseline evaluation, source-fitted shift diagnostics, and conventional evidence export. It preserves denominators, missing hypotheses/classes and provenance. It does not solve harmonization, authenticate donors, adjust covariates or establish causality. The ER public pair is eligible for exploratory association replication, not direct-scale comparison or model transfer.

## Nontechnical explanation

BRACE helps researchers check whether a finding from one group of samples still has support in another. You can see which findings hold up, which are uncertain, and which could not be tested. You can inspect the records behind a model error and take away a report with its evidence and limits.
